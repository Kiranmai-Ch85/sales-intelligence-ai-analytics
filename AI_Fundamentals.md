# AI Fundamentals for Business Analytics

## 1. AI and Machine Learning Fundamentals

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to techniques that enable computer systems to perform tasks that normally require human-like reasoning, pattern recognition, or decision-making.

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI in which models learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions or classifications.

### Common Machine Learning Types

- **Supervised Learning** — learns from labeled data.
- **Unsupervised Learning** — identifies patterns or groups in unlabeled data.
- **Classification** — predicts a category or class.
- **Regression** — predicts a numerical value.
- **Clustering** — groups similar records based on their characteristics.

### Algorithms Relevant to Data Analysis

- Linear Regression
- Logistic Regression
- Decision Trees
- K-Means Clustering

In this project, AI/ML concepts are considered from a beginner-level business analytics perspective rather than an AI engineering perspective.

## 2. Generative AI Fundamentals

Generative AI refers to AI systems that can generate new content such as text, summaries, explanations, code, or structured responses based on user instructions.

### Generative AI Use Cases for Data Analysts

- Summarizing analytical findings
- Explaining SQL queries
- Generating ideas for data-quality checks
- Creating first drafts of documentation
- Converting business questions into analytical questions
- Explaining charts and trends in simple language

### Important Limitations

Generative AI output should be reviewed before being used for business decisions. Generated information can contain incorrect assumptions, calculations, or interpretations.

For analytical work, important results should be validated against the underlying data and actual calculations.

## 3. Prompt Engineering Fundamentals

Prompt engineering means designing clear instructions so that an AI system can produce a more useful and structured response.

### Basic Prompt Structure

A useful analytical prompt can include:

1. **Role** — what the AI should act as
2. **Task** — what needs to be done
3. **Context** — relevant business or dataset information
4. **Constraints** — limitations or requirements
5. **Output format** — how the response should be presented

### Example

**Basic prompt:**

> Analyze these sales results and identify important trends.

**More structured prompt:**

> Act as a business data analyst. Analyze the provided sales results, identify the main revenue and profitability trends, highlight unusual changes, and present the findings as five concise business insights. Do not invent values that are not present in the data.

---

## 4. AI-Assisted Analytics Workflow

A basic AI-assisted analytics workflow can be:

**Business Question → Data Preparation → Analysis → AI-Assisted Interpretation → Validation → Business Insight**

AI can assist with explanations and documentation, but the underlying data analysis and important results should be independently checked.

---

## 5. Responsible Use of AI in Analytics

When using AI with business data:

- Validate generated calculations and conclusions.
- Avoid entering confidential or sensitive information into unapproved AI tools.
- Clearly distinguish AI-generated suggestions from verified analytical results.
- Use the original dataset and analytical calculations as the source of truth.
