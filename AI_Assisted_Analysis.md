# AI-Assisted Business Analysis

## Objective

Use beginner-level AI concepts and prompt engineering to support business analysis while keeping the actual data calculations and conclusions grounded in the project data.

## Business Question

Which factors appear to have the strongest relationship with sales performance and profitability?

## Structured Analysis Prompt

> Act as a business data analyst. Based only on the available sales-analysis results, identify important patterns related to product category, region, discount level, customer value, and time period. Summarize the findings as concise business insights. Do not invent values that are not present in the data.

## AI-Assisted Analysis Approach

The AI can be used to help:

- Structure business questions
- Suggest analytical angles
- Summarize already-calculated findings
- Explain SQL queries or dashboard metrics
- Convert technical findings into simple business language

The underlying calculations should still be performed using the project's Python, SQL, and Power BI analysis.

## Validation

AI-generated interpretations should be checked against the actual dataset, SQL results, Python analysis, and Power BI measures before being treated as verified business findings.

## Example Prompt Engineering Pattern

**Role:** Business data analyst  
**Task:** Identify important sales and profitability patterns  
**Context:** Retail sales analysis  
**Constraint:** Use only verified project results  
**Output:** Five concise business insights

## Key Principle

AI is used as an **analysis-support tool**, not as a replacement for data validation, statistical reasoning, SQL analysis, or business judgment.
