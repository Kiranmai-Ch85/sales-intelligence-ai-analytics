KEY FINDINGS - Smart Sales Intelligence Dashboard
==================================================

Dataset: 5,000 orders | 2021-2024 | 300 customers | 50 products
Total revenue: about Rs. 6.48 Crore
Total profit: about Rs. 1.39 Crore
Overall profit margin: 21.5%


1. Electronics makes the most money but has the worst margin
   - Electronics is about 78% of total revenue but only an 18% profit margin,
     the lowest of all categories.
   - It sells in high value but heavy discounting eats into the profit.
   - Clothing, by contrast, has the best margin at ~41%.


2. Discounts hurt profit a lot
   - Orders with no discount average a ~40% margin.
   - Orders with more than 30% discount drop to only ~14% margin.
   - So the more we discount, the thinner the profit gets - the drop is steep.


3. Q4 is the biggest sales quarter
   - October to December brings in about 29% of the whole year's revenue.
   - This lines up with the festive season (Diwali, Christmas, year-end).


4. West region is the most profitable
   - West has the highest profit margin (~23%), even though North has the
     highest total revenue.
   - So more revenue does not always mean more profit - margin matters.


5. A small group of customers drives a big share of revenue
   - The top 20 customers (out of 300) bring in about 15% of total revenue.
   - This is the usual pattern where a few customers matter the most.


Simple takeaways
----------------
- Be careful with discounts on Electronics - they cut into profit.
- Stock up and push marketing before Q4.
- Look at what West region is doing right and try it in other regions.
- Keep the top customers happy since they bring a large share of revenue.
